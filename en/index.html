Why Orig?

In origami, the first folds look like nothing. Yet they make all the precision of the final figure. I work the same way: time to observe and understand, then a tool that fits the way you work without disrupting it.

In a world where everything moves fast, I take the time.

Foundations

Like in origami, everything happens in the first folds. I don't just follow a specification: I write it with you by watching how you work, and I add what you never suspected computing could do for you. The tool then fits your habits instead of imposing new ones.

Accessibility

Tools that adapt to people, not the other way round. WCAG 2.2 level AA, AAA criteria wherever possible: strong contrast, large click targets, full keyboard use, reduced motion on request, French and English.

Privacy

You are not being followed: no cookies, no audience measurement, no advertising. My software works on your own computer: OrigPDF never sends your documents anywhere.

Transparency

No trap interfaces, no hidden fees. Prices are displayed, so are the limits of my software, and every download comes with its verification checksum.

Approach
