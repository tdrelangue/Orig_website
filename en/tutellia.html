Software for court-appointed guardians (MJPM)

Your case-file paperwork, filed and sent without a second thought.

Tutellia files every document in the right case, for the right person, and sends it to the right recipient. You handle it once, when you are already working on it, then forget about it.

See Tutellia pricing Download Tutellia

How it works

Handle it once, forget it, find it ready.

  1. 01

    You receive

    A bill, a statement or a letter arrives. While you log it in your usual case software, drag it into the right Tutellia case file. Done: you can forget about it.

  2. 02

    Tutellia files

    For ASH (housing social aid), drop all your tables at once. Each PDF is read, matched to the right person and period, and given a clean name. If the case file does not exist yet, it is created.

  3. 03

    Tutellia sends

    When the time comes, everything leaves for the right recipients, in standardised emails: the person's name, the period, the attachments. Nothing to type again.

Features

Built for the daily work of an MJPM

  • APA (personal autonomy allowance) and ASH modules, with linked case files when a person is followed in both
  • Automatic sorting of ASH PDFs by person and period
  • Email templates with variables: name, month, year
  • Bulk sending, or one case file at a time
  • Closing emails ready for death or discharge
  • Priorities, search and filters on every case file
  • Full history: what was sent, to whom, when
  • Automatic, signature-verified updates

Installation and verification

Windows

The installer is not yet signed with a publisher certificate: if Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg file and drag Tutellia into Applications.
  2. Tutellia is not yet notarized by Apple. The first time you open it, macOS blocks it: go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. On macOS 14 and earlier, right-click the app › Open is enough.
  3. If macOS says the app “is damaged”, run this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tutellia.app
